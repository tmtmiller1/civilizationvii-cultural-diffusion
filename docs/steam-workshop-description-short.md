Your civilization's cultural pressure becomes a living field that spreads from your cities tile by tile in Civilization VII. A strong, established civilization slowly grows its borders past the normal city footprint, seeps into open land, and can flip a rival's frontier tiles once its cultural pressure there decisively wins — a systemic answer to AI forward-settling. ("Cultural pressure" is the mod's own per-tile quantity, not the base game's Culture yield — the Culture yield is just one input into it.) Growth is slow and organic: a reaction-diffusion field where cities inject pressure, it diffuses along roads and rivers (slowed by rough terrain), decays each turn, and flips ownership only where one civilization clearly dominates, so borders creep outward over the game and, with the opt-in recede mode, can be lost to a rival whose pressure overtakes yours. Claimed land lies beyond your cities' first three rings, where Civ VII does not let a city work tiles, so it is territory rather than yield. Reach is driven by cultural power — a fused blend of the Culture yield, prosperity, and a per-civilization Cultural Power Index — not raw Culture alone, so a happy, entrenched civilization reaches farther without any single stat running away. Real gameplay writes, not a UI-only layer. Single-player only, with Low/Medium/High intensity and an off switch for vanilla borders.

A reimagining and extension of Gedemon's Civ V Cultural Diffusion mod, rebuilt for Civ VII. Works fully standalone, and reads diaspora data from the Emigration mod when it is installed so your borders can follow your people.

Like its companion Emigration, this mod is a work in progress: balance is still being tuned and more is planned, including opt-in conquest-based tile flips and fuller Options-menu parity with Emigration. Feedback welcome.
